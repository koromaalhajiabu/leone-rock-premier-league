Reports about my club

Reports the league office has shared with your club. Reply to each one. The league office will see your reply. The reporter's name is withheld from clubs. Do not try to find out who reported or treat any player differently.

All player reports

Only the Super Admin can share a report with a club or close it. The reporter's name is visible only to the Super Admin and the Developer. Clubs and teammates never see it.

Confidentiality rule: share only what the club needs to respond. Do not forward, copy or discuss a report, or the reporter's name, outside this system. Do not tell teammates who reported.
